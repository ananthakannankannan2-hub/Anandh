Scene 01

About

New enough that every small thing that works still feels like a plot twist.— Anantha, first year

I'm Anantha Kannan, a first-year B.Tech student in Information Technology. I'm at the very start of the story, and I've decided that's a good place to be: everything is new, and nothing has had the chance to feel boring yet.

I got drawn to building things on screens because of how much you can say with a few lines of code. A page that loads, a button that does what it promises, a layout that feels like someone sat down and thought about it. That last part matters to me as much as the code underneath.

Right now I'm learning the basics properly instead of rushing past them. I'd rather understand why something works than copy it and hope. I like asking questions, I'm happy to say "I don't know that yet", and I'm looking for people and projects I can learn from.

This site is dressed as a film title sequence on purpose. If you stay until the end credits, you'll find the rest of me.
